# Manuscript Outline

## Title Candidates

- CIRCUIT-ICB identifies pan-cancer malignant-cell resistance states and druggable vulnerabilities from public single-cell and perturbational multi-omics data
- CIRCUIT-ICB: A cell-state-resolved and perturbation-aware pan-cancer atlas of immune checkpoint blockade resistance

## Abstract Skeleton

Background:
Immune checkpoint blockade has variable efficacy, and transcriptomic biomarkers often generalize poorly across cohorts and cancer types.

Question:
Can malignant-cell states from ICB-treated patients provide transferable biomarkers and nominate druggable resistance vulnerabilities?

Data:
Public ICB-treated scRNA-seq, bulk ICB response cohorts, TCGA/GDC, CPTAC/PDC, DepMap, PRISM/GDSC, LINCS/CLUE, GTEx/HPA, and optional spatial transcriptomics.

Methods:
Malignant-cell programs are discovered from scRNA-seq, aggregated to patient level, transferred to bulk RNA-seq by rank-based state scores, benchmarked across cohorts, and linked to target/drug evidence through a transparent scoring system.

Results:
To be filled after analysis.

Conclusion:
CIRCUIT-ICB provides an open framework for mapping ICB resistance states to biomarkers and candidate combination strategies.
